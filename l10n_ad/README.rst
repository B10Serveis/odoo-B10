This module provides the Andorran accounting localization layer for Odoo 17. It adapts Odoo Accounting to businesses operating in Andorra with an Andorran chart of accounts, account groups, tax templates, and fiscal positions.

The module includes:

* An Andorran chart of accounts and its account groups.
* IGI sales and purchase tax templates at 0%, 1%, 2.5%, 4.5%, and 9.5%, with tax groups and accounting repartition accounts.
* IRPF withholding tax templates at 5% and 10% for sales and purchases.
* General and international fiscal position templates.
* Company accounting defaults, including receivable and payable accounts, bank and cash accounts, journals, and default taxes.
* A technical field for identifying simplified invoices in accounting documents.

This module provides the accounting setup and tax templates. It does not generate IGI returns or electronic tax filings.

Installation and Usage
======================

Install **Andorra - Accounting** from the Apps menu, or install the ``l10n_ad`` module with your usual Odoo deployment process. The Accounting application is required.

During accounting setup, set the company country to Andorra and select the Andorra chart template. Odoo then creates the chart of accounts, account groups, tax groups, taxes, and fiscal positions for the company.

The company defaults to the general 4.5% IGI sales and purchase taxes. Review the default accounts and taxes, then assign the appropriate taxes to products and services for the company's activities. Use the provided fiscal positions when they match the transaction and customer situation; review their mappings before using them for live transactions.

The account and tax templates are a starting point and can be adapted to the company's accounting practices.
